The menu: moments on the line, and the places that stay

A proposal for you to agree before R1. Today's sixteen menu items become six places, a short More list and Settings. Anything that is "something happened" or "something needs you" becomes a moment on the line instead of a place you go and look.

2 · The decision card (R2)

One card at a time, the whole action on screen before Approve is live, and Approve is held, not tapped. The four facts come from code, never from the model. "Always allow" is offered only where promote.ts may propose it: never for an always-confirm tool or a call made after outside content was read.
